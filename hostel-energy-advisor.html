What should my file contain?

One row for every 30 minutes, with these columns (names can differ slightly):

Needed: date and time, total electricity used (kWh), solar electricity made (kWh).
Helpful, not required: price per kWh, and kWh for laundry, water pumps, water heaters, air conditioners. The more you add, the more ideas we can test.

You can drop several files at once if they share the same columns. Your data stays in this browser and is never uploaded.

Working on it

    These are estimates from a simplified model that runs in your browser. They show where the savings are and roughly how big they are. Prices for a final purchase decision should come from supplier quotes.
